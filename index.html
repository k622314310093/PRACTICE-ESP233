Kho Mẫu Câu & Công Thức Viết Theo TACN.pdf

Được trích xuất trực tiếp từ quy định môn ESP233 TC-NH. Đảm bảo tuân thủ cấu trúc paragraph bắt buộc.

Dạng 1

Agree or Disagree

QUY TẮC BẮT BUỘC: Phải chọn 1 quan điểm duy nhất (1 position). KHÔNG viết Partly Agree/Disagree.

1. Introduction:

• Hook: Introduce the topic in your own words.

• Background info: Context & explaining keywords.

• Thesis Statement: "For reasons A and B, I firmly agree/disagree with the opinion that..."

2. Body Paragraphs (2 or 3 Main Points):

• Topic Sentence: Nêu ý chính (General statement, not too specific).

• Supporting Sentences (≥ 3 câu): Dùng câu ghép / câu phức để giải thích lý do, tác động.

• Concluding statement (Optional): Restatement dùng Thus, Therefore...

3. Conclusion:

• Thesis restatement: Sum up main points (dùng từ đồng nghĩa A' B') + Re-state position.

• Final sentence: A piece of advice / A warning / An insight of writer.

Dạng 2

Advantages & Disadvantages

Bên nào nhiều hơn thì chiếm 2 đoạn Body. Bên ít hơn 1 đoạn.

Mẫu Thesis Statement Intro:

"However, there are both pros and cons to [Topic]. In this essay, I shall discuss [the benefits] and some of these challenges to be overcome."

Mẫu Conclusion Formula:

"In conclusion, there are both benefits and drawbacks of [Topic] among which A', B' and C' are the most noteworthy to be taken into account. All things considered, ... Personally, I believe the benefits in terms of [X] eventually outweigh any negatives."

Dạng 3

Discuss Both Views

Cấu Trúc Body (3 Paragraphs):

• Body 1: Analyze View 1 (The arguments supporting the first perspective).

• Body 2: Analyze View 2 (The opposing viewpoint).

• Body 3: Your own view (Align with View 1, View 2, or present a synthesis).

Mẫu Intro Thesis:

"I shall discuss both views above and state my own viewpoint on this issue."

Dạng 4

Cause/Problem - Solution

Cấu Trúc Đoạn Body Tương Ứng:

• Body 1: Main Cause 1 + Solution(s) 1

• Body 2: Main Cause 2 + Solution(s) 2

Mẫu Kết Bài Chuẩn TACN.pdf:

"To sum up, [Problem] is caused due to reasons such as A' and B'. This is a serious problem unless we can [take action]. My view is that the main responsibility for solving the issue lies with [Solutions X, Y]."
